Tape-Measure Fractions

Type a number, then tap a fraction

Type the whole number, then tap its fraction: 25 then ½ is 25 ½. Or type a decimal like 12.83 to get the nearest tape mark. Tap ft ' after a number for feet, like 3 ft ' 4 ½. ⌫ takes back only the last thing you pressed. After =, tap an operator to keep going from the answer.

Pipe Fitting

Measure in

Simple offset

Fitting angle

Travel = offset × cosecant of the angle. Run (advance) = offset × cotangent. Multipliers: 45° 1.414, 22½° 2.613, 60° 1.155, 30° 2.000, 11¼° 5.126.

Rolling offset

Fitting angle

True offset = √(rise² + roll²). Travel = true offset × cosecant. Setback = true offset × cotangent.

Elbow take-outs (butt-weld)

Fitting

Center-to-end per ASME B16.9 (same for every schedule). Long radius = 1.5 × NPS, short radius = 1 × NPS. B16.9 has short radius only from 1" up, and no 45° short radius.

Cut length

Welds

Cut = center to center − take-out at each end − (gap × welds).

Miter cuts (lobster back)

Pieces

Cut angle = total bend ÷ (2 × welds). Cut-back = OD ÷ 2 × tan(cut angle), marked each side of the centerline. Middle piece: 2 × radius × tan on the centerline, add OD × tan on the long side, take it off the short side. End pieces are half that, plus any straight you want.

Answers round to the nearest 1/16". Check against your drawing before you cut.
